About

Made by people, for people.

We're a community interest company based in Southall, running events across Ealing in partnership with Peabody and Ealing Council. We run weekly movement and art sessions, and bring neighbours together through parties, workshops and community-run events.

Sessions are led by experienced practitioners in accessible spaces. Timings are flexible, refreshments are on, and nobody needs experience. Everyone has a story to share through movement.

Always free Every session and event, funded for the community.
Little ones & grown-ups Weekly movement and art for under-5s with their parents and carers.
Everyone welcome Parties, workshops and community-run events for all ages and abilities.
